# Data Storage & Platforms

This section provides an overview of the data storage platforms, tools, and services available at TUM for managing research data throughout its lifecycle - from active collaboration and sharing to long-term preservation.

## How to use this diagram

- **Hot Data** refers to data you are actively working with — it needs quick access, frequent updates, and often collaboration.
- **Cold Data** refers to data that is no longer actively used but needs to be preserved for legal or scientific reasons.
- **Hover over the "?" icons** to learn more about each tool, including storage limits and key features.
- Follow the **decision flow** in the Cold Data section to determine the right preservation strategy for your research data.

For more details on individual tools, refer to the respective pages in this Knowledge Hub.
